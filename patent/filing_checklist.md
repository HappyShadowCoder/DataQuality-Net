## 1. STATUTORY FILING OVERVIEW & CRITICAL TIMELINES

Filing a **Provisional Patent Specification** establishes an official **Priority Date** with the Indian Patent Office (IPO / CGPDTM - Controller General of Patents, Designs and Trade Marks). This safeguards the invention prior to public disclosure, academic publishing, conference presentation, or open-source release.

| Statutory Milestone | Governing Rule / Section | Critical Deadline | Action Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Provisional Application Filing** | Section 9(1), Patents Act 1970 | **Day 0 (Priority Date)** | Submit Form 1, Form 2 (Provisional Specification), Form 3, Form 5, Form 28. |
| **Complete Specification Filing** | Section 9(1), Rule 13 | **Within 12 Months from Priority Date** *(Strict Statutory Bar)* | Submit Complete Specification with definitive final claims. **Cannot be extended.** Failure to file results in abandonment. |
| **Publication of Application** | Section 11A, Rule 24 | **18 Months from Priority Date** (or within 1 month via Form 9 early publication) | Official publication in the Patent Office Journal. Application becomes public record. |
| **Request for Examination (RFE)** | Section 11B, Rule 24B | **Within 48 Months from Priority Date** (or 31 months under draft rules) | Submit Form 18 or Form 18A (Expedited Examination for Startups / Women Inventors / Educational Institutes). |
| **First Examination Report (FER)** | Section 12, Rule 24B | Typically within 6–18 months from RFE | IPO examiner issues objections (novelty, inventive step, Section 3(k)). |
| **Response to FER** | Rule 24B(5) | **Within 6 Months from FER Issuance** (Extendable by 3 months via Form 4) | File written response and amended claims addressing objections. |

## 2. STATUTORY FORMS CHECKLIST

Every filing package submitted through the comprehensive e-filing portal ([ipindiaonline.gov.in](https://ipindiaonline.gov.in)) must include the following statutory forms:

### Form 1: Application for Grant of Patent
- **Governing Law:** Section 7, 54, 135 and Rule 20(1).
- **Key Details to Enter:**
  - Type of Application: Ordinary Application — Provisional.
  - Applicant Category: Natural Person / Startup / Educational Institution.
  - Inventor Name & Nationality: **Swastiek Kala** (Indian).
  - Applicant Address for Service in India: Must be a valid postal address within India with pin code.
  - Title of Invention: Exact match with Form 2.
  - Declaration of Inventorship: Executed by all inventors.

### Form 2: Provisional Specification
- **Governing Law:** Section 10 and Rule 13.
- **Components to Attach:**
  - Preamble ("THE FOLLOWING SPECIFICATION DESCRIBES THE INVENTION").
  - Title of Invention.
  - Description: Field, Background, Objects, Summary, Brief Description of Drawings, Detailed Description, Working Examples, Alternative Embodiments.
  - Provisional Claims (Recommended to anchor priority scope).
  - Abstract of Invention (max 150 words).
  - Formal Drawings (FIG. 1 to FIG. 6).
- **Document Source:** Formatted directly from `patent/provisional_specification.md` and `patent/drawings/figure_descriptions.md`.

### Form 3: Statement and Undertaking under Section 8
- **Governing Law:** Section 8 and Rule 12.
- **Purpose:** Undertaking to inform the Controller of any foreign patent applications filed for the same or substantially the same invention (e.g., PCT, USPTO, EPO).
- **Action:** If no foreign filings are initiated at Day 0, check the negative declaration ("Applicant has not made any foreign application").

### Form 5: Declaration as to Inventorship
- **Governing Law:** Rule 13(6).
- **Purpose:** Formal declaration that the named individuals are the true and first inventors.
- **Timing:** Can be filed along with Form 2 or within the prescribed period following the Complete Specification. It is best practice to submit at the time of provisional filing.

### Form 28: Proof of Category (Small Entity / Startup / Educational Institute)
- **Governing Law:** Rule 2(fa), 2(fb), and Rule 7.
- **Benefit:** Reduces official government patent fees by **up to 80%**.
- **Required Supporting Documents:**
  - For Startups: DPIIT Recognition Certificate.
  - For Micro/Small Enterprises (MSME): Udyam Registration Certificate.
  - For Educational Institutions: Official university/college registration certificate.

## 4. E-FILING STEP-BY-STEP PROCEDURE (PORTAL: ipindiaonline.gov.in)

1. **Digital Signature Certificate (DSC):**
   - The applicant or registered patent agent must hold a valid Class 3 DSC (Signing & Encryption) issued by a licensed Certifying Authority in India.
2. **User Account Registration:**
   - Create an e-filing login at [ipindiaonline.gov.in](https://ipindiaonline.gov.in) mapped to the Class 3 DSC token.
3. **Draft Compilation:**
   - Export `patent/provisional_specification.md` into standard PDF format (A4 page size, 1.5 line spacing, 12pt font, minimum 2.5cm margins).
   - Ensure drawings (`patent/drawings/`) are rendered with clear black lines on neutral white background without color fills or photo textures.
4. **Uploading Forms:**
   - Log into the portal and select *New Application -> Ordinary Application -> Provisional*.
   - Upload Form 1, Form 2 (PDF), Form 3, Form 5, and Form 28 (with supporting certificate).
5. **Electronic Signing & Payment:**
   - Electronically sign each generated form using the DSC token.
   - Pay the statutory government fees through the Bharatkosh / integrated payment gateway.
   - Download the official **Filing Receipt and Application Number** (format: `YYYY-CC-NNNNNN` e.g., `202611012345`).

2. **Preserve Complete Development Logs:**

3. **12-Month Complete Specification Roadmap:**
   - Prior to Month 12, execute further multi-modal benchmarks (time-series and image metadata) as described in the alternative embodiments.
   - Finalize formal non-dependent claims, referencing the exact physical apparatus and transformation effects to preempt Section 3(k) objections.
